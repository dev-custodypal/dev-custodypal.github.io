We had a fab day on Jekyll Island. We paid $20 to use the dinghy dock at Jekyll Island Marina and that gave us access to their pool and bikes!

We rode to the little downtown where Sage played at the splash pad. Then we rode over to the Sea Turtle Center where they nurse injured or sick turtles back to health. 

After that we rode into the historic district and had a jar or two at an outdoor bar called Founders Social. 

All in all, a pretty awesome day.
